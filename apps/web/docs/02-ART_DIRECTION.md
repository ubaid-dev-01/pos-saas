# QuickPOS — Art Direction Guide

**Companion to:** `01-VISUAL_DIRECTION.md`  
**Status:** Research / direction only. No UI build.

---

## 1. Creative territory

**Name:** *Counter Truth*

**One-liner:**  
The operating system for the retail floor—presented with the calm authority of a financial product and the honesty of a well-run shop.

**Mood board keywords (principles, not images to copy):**  
Quiet confidence · ledger clarity · receipt paper · barcode moment · cool daylight · editorial crop · instrument not poster.

**Emotional register:**  
Mercury/Ramp trust × Square counter × Apple chaptering × Stripe diagram intelligence.

---

## 2. Art direction pillars

### Pillar A — The product is the art
Every major section’s “hero object” is a **truthful QuickPOS surface** (checkout, inventory, receipt, report) framed editorially.  
We do not decorate around an absent product.

### Pillar B — Commerce realism
Visuals show *work*: scanning, tender, stock counts, WhatsApp tickets.  
Not founders on beanbags. Not abstract “innovation.”

### Pillar C — Expensive quiet
Premium is communicated by **what we omit**: fewer sections, larger air, one accent, no stickers, no icon parks.

### Pillar D — Systems you can see
When we explain architecture (sale → stock → customer → report), we **draw the system** with hairline precision—Stripe essay energy, original geometry.

## 4. Photography direction

**Grade:** Cool, slightly desaturated, match paper `#F4F6F8`-ish canvas.  
**Lens feel:** 35–50mm documentary; shallow DOF only if product remains readable.  
**Subjects:** Counters, shelves, scanners, printed receipts, storefronts at golden-blue hour.  
**People:** Hands/work preferred over smiling portraits.  
**Crop:** Editorial, consistent aspect (e.g. 3:2 or 16:10) across a page.

---

## 5. Product staging direction

**Chrome:** Minimal window chrome (three dots optional) OR flush UI on inset surface.  
**Inset surface:** Cool gray recess—not floating card with heavy shadow.  
**Data:** Believable PKR amounts, real category names (Nestlé, Tapal)—not “Acme Corp $2.4M.”  
**Lighting:** Flat UI lighting; no fake screen glow.  
**Scale:** Large enough to read primary actions (Charge, qty, total).

---

## 6. Diagram direction

- 1.5–2px strokes  
- Corner radius 0–4px (sharp = serious)  
- Labels in 12px caps or mono  
- Accent only on the “active” hop  
- Plenty of margin; diagram never touches section edges  

Tone: **architecture drawing**, not infographic clipart.

---

## 7. Color application (art rules)

| Surface | Use |
|---------|-----|
| Paper canvas | Default marketing field |
| White | Stages, forms, elevated interactive |
| Ink | Dark chapters (solution, testimonials, close)—max 2–3 per long page |
| Signal accent | CTA, focus ring, diagram active state, success in UI |
| Hairline | Structure without boxing everything into “cards” |

**Card rule:** Default = no card. Use border only when it aids interaction or table structure. If removing shadow/radius doesn’t hurt → remove.

---

## 8. Typography art direction

- One grotesque family sitewide for marketing.  
- Headlines: confident but **subordinate to the visual**.  
- Brand lockup: consistent mark geometry; wordmark never rainbow.  
- Avoid: costume display fonts, mixed three-font stacks, huge single-word heroes with no product.

**Voice in type:** Short. Concrete. Operator language (“stock that tells the truth”)—not “supercharge your synergy.”

---

## 9. Motion art direction

Motion is **editorial**, like a documentary cut:
- Enter: opacity + small Y (≤16px), 400–600ms, ease-out  
- Diagram: sequential reveal of nodes  
- Stage tabs: crossfade, no spin  

No Lottie unless it is a **literal workflow** (scan beam → line item). Decorative Lottie = fail.

---

## 10. Sound of the brand (visual metaphor)

If QuickPOS were an object: **a clean metal barcode scanner on a wooden counter**—precise, tool-like, not cute.

If QuickPOS were a publication: **a finance journal insert**—tables, diagrams, one excellent photo.

---

## 11. Competitive art contrast

| Generic AI SaaS | Counter Truth |
|-----------------|---------------|
| Purple gradient hero | Cool paper + product stage |
| 3 feature cards with icons | One diagram or one stage |
| Floating 3D laptop | Editorial UI flush on inset |
| Marquee logos | Quiet metrics or local cases |
| Long feature walls | Few deep chapters |
| Bounce animations | Explain-only motion |

---

## 12. Review checklist (senior designer gate)

For every section before build:

1. What is the single visual moment?  
2. Does it increase trust or explain the product?  
3. Would Figma/Stripe reject the decoration level?  
4. Is text supporting or competing?  
5. Does spacing snap to 8pt with clear rhythm?  
6. Is the composition balanced when blurred?  

If any answer fails → redesign the **visual**, not add more copy.
